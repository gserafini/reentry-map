# Reentry Map usability validation

Prepared September 18, 2026. Tracker: reentry_map T002. This is a study plan, not participant evidence.

## Goal

Find out whether someone can locate relevant support, understand whether it might fit, and leave with a usable next step. A telephone-link click is an attempt to connect, not evidence of service receipt.

## Participants and setup

Invite a small group of returning community members and people who help them once Gabriel identifies an appropriate group and authorizes outreach. Participation is optional. Use fictional situations; do not ask for conviction details, medical history, exact home location, or other sensitive information. Record observations without names. Ask before recording audio or video.

Include a low-cost Android phone, an iPhone, a slow or interrupted connection, keyboard navigation, and a screen reader. Browser emulation supplements these devices; it does not replace them.

## Tasks to read aloud

1. Imagine you will be in Dallas next week and need work. Find a service you could contact. Explain what it offers and what you would do next.
2. Imagine you need a place to sleep near Lubbock. Find an option, then tell us what you know and what still needs confirmation.
3. Find 7More from Dallas. Explain whether the organization serves Dallas and whether the map indicates somewhere to walk in.
4. Narrow results to Housing. Open an organization, return to results, and show us where you left off.
5. Save two options without making an account. Reload the page, find the saved list, and produce a copy you could take with you.
6. Disconnect from the network and retrieve a saved telephone number. Reconnect and return to the directory.
7. Search for a service we cannot find in the selected area. Find another useful next step without losing the selected place.
8. Imagine a displayed telephone number is wrong. Find how to report it. Stop before submitting a real report.
9. Imagine this is a shared phone. Remove saved information and explain what has been cleared.

Do not coach the participant toward a particular button. If they get stuck, ask what they expected to happen before offering help.

## Observations

For each task, record completion, time to the first useful action, hesitation, backtracking, misunderstood labels, and whether the participant can explain the next step. Record the device, connection, and assistive technology used. Distinguish a verified behavior from an inference.

Proposed design goals to validate: a useful result and contact action within the initial phone screen; correct geographic scope after sharing, refresh, and return; no inferred availability or walk-in claims; successful guest saving and offline contact retrieval.

## Synthesis and follow-through

Enter each significant finding in the existing reentry_map PM tracker. Fix high-impact confusion, rerun the affected task, and preserve before/after evidence. Do not mark T002 complete until actual participants have completed the study and its findings have been reviewed.

Automated release verification covers these same journeys on phone/tablet/desktop and records browser console errors, request failures, and screenshots. It is engineering evidence, not a substitute for this study.
